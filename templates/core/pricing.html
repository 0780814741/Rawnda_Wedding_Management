Pricing and fees

Using the planner is free. When you accept an offer, the total you pay is:

  • the vendors' prices for the services in your offer, plus
  • our disclosed service fee (currently {{ PLATFORM_SERVICE_FEE_PERCENT }}% of the vendor subtotal).

Plans also show a contingency reserve. It is a suggested buffer for unexpected costs. It is not charged unless it is actually needed and agreed with you.

We may also receive a commission from vendors for bookings. Human-assisted planning is available as an optional service; its price will be told to you before you agree.

Prices on vendor pages and plans are indicative until the vendor confirms them to our team.
