How it works

  1. Set up your wedding. Budget, date, district, guest count and ceremonies.
  2. Review suggested plans. We show a few combinations of services within your budget, and explain trade-offs.
  3. Send your request. Your request goes to our team, not directly to vendors.
  4. We check with vendors. Our team confirms availability and prices, outside this website, so you do not need to negotiate.
  5. Review your offer. You get one quotation with a validity date. Accept or decline it on your dashboard.
  6. Pay as instructed, then bookings are confirmed. A booking is confirmed only when the vendor confirms it, and a payment counts only after we verify it.
Estimates are produced by fixed planning rules and vendor package prices. They are not guarantees. Always check whether a price is indicative, vendor-confirmed or a confirmed booking.
