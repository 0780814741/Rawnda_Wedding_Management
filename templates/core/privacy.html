Privacy notice

What we collect

Your name, email, optional phone number, and your wedding details (date, district, guest count, budget, preferences). We do not ask for guest names or lists.

How we use it

To prepare your plan and quotation, and to contact vendors for you. When we contact vendors we share only the date, place, guest count and service needs, not your personal contact details.

Vendor details

Vendor phone numbers and emails are kept private by our team.

Keeping and deleting data

You can ask us to delete your account from the Account page. We delete or anonymise personal data after a request, and remove inactive draft plans after a retention period. Invoices and payment records may be kept for accounting, without more personal data than necessary.

Payments

We never ask for card numbers, PINs or passwords. Only pay using the official instructions shown on your invoice in this website.
